01 · GENERATION

Solar Panels

Photovoltaic cells convert sunlight into DC electricity.

What it does: Uses silicon photovoltaic (PV) cells to absorb sunlight photons and knock electrons loose, producing Direct Current (DC) electricity without any moving parts or pollution.
02 · MEASUREMENT

Electricity Meter

Records electricity imported from and exported to the grid.

What it does: Tracks both the grid electricity your home consumes at night (Import) and the surplus solar electricity sent back to the utility grid during sunny afternoons (Export) for bill credits.
03 · PROTECTION

Earthing System

Part of the electrical protection and safety arrangement.

What it does: Provides a low-resistance path deep into the soil to safely discharge lightning strikes, static electricity, and short-circuit leakage currents—protecting both family members and expensive appliances.
04 · CONNECTION

Electrical Wiring

Connects the system components for electricity transfer.

What it does: Uses UV-resistant double-insulated DC solar cables, MC4 waterproof connectors, and ACDB/DCDB surge protection boxes to transfer power safely with minimal voltage drop.
05 · CONVERSION

Solar Inverter

Converts DC electricity from the panels into AC electricity.

What it does: Acts as the brain of the solar system—converting DC power from rooftop panels into 230V 50Hz Alternating Current (AC) for home fans, lights, and TVs while synchronizing safely with the grid.

PHYSICS BEHIND THE PROJECT

Scientific Principle

The science that makes solar electricity possible.

1. Photovoltaic Effect

The photovoltaic effect is the production of an electric potential difference when light falls on a suitable semiconductor material. Solar cells use this effect to convert sunlight into electrical energy.

2. Semiconductor Material

Most conventional solar cells use silicon, a semiconductor. Its electrical properties can be controlled by adding small amounts of suitable impurities, a process called doping.

3. P-N Junction

A solar cell contains p-type and n-type semiconductor regions. Their junction creates an internal electric field that helps separate light-generated charge carriers.

4. Electron-Hole Pairs

Photons with sufficient energy can create electron-hole pairs. The internal electric field separates these charges, helping produce current through an external circuit.

Scientific Flow

Sunlight → Photon absorption → Electron-hole pair generation → Charge separation → DC electrical current.

E = hν

Here, E is photon energy, h is Planck's constant and ν is frequency of light.

SOLAR TECHNOLOGIES & CAPACITIES

Types of Solar Panels & Systems

Explore panel technologies, capacity sizes (1 kW to 10 kW+), and On-Grid, Off-Grid & Hybrid architectures. Click + to learn more.

PANEL TYPE · 21%–23% EFFICIENCY

Monocrystalline PERC Panels

Sleek black high-efficiency silicon panels ideal for Indian rooftops with limited space.

Made from single-crystal silicon ingots. They generate more electricity per square foot and perform better in hot and low-light morning/evening conditions.
NEXT-GEN · DUAL SIDE

Bifacial N-Type TOPCon Panels

Captures sunlight from both the front glass and reflected light from the white rooftop underneath.

Produces 10%–15% bonus generation from reflected ground albedo, comes with a 30-year performance warranty, and suffers minimal heat degradation.
SYSTEM TYPE · SUBSIDY ELIGIBLE

On-Grid Solar System (1kW – 10kW)

Connected directly to the electricity grid with net metering. Eligible for PM Surya Ghar subsidy.

No expensive batteries required. Excess daytime units are exported to the grid and credited to your monthly electricity bill, giving the fastest 3–4 year payback.
SYSTEM TYPE · BATTERY BACKUP

Off-Grid Solar System

Independent solar power plant with tubular or lithium batteries for areas with frequent power cuts.

Stores daytime solar energy in a battery bank so fans, lights, and Wi-Fi continue running seamlessly at night or during utility blackouts without needing a grid line.
SYSTEM TYPE · ALL-IN-ONE

Hybrid Solar System

Combines grid export benefits with lithium battery backup for uninterrupted 24×7 power.

First charges your batteries, then runs household loads, and finally exports extra units to the grid—giving you both electricity bill savings and blackout protection.
CAPACITY GUIDE · 1 kW TO 10 kW

System Sizes by kW Rating

1–2 kW for small homes, 3–5 kW for AC households, and 8–10 kW+ for large villas & shops.

1 kW generates ~120 units/month (~80–100 sq.ft roof). A 3 kW system generates ~360 units/month and receives up to ₹78,000 central government subsidy.

PANEL CARE & SAFETY

Solar Maintenance & Cleaning Guide

Dust and bird droppings can reduce solar output by 15%–25%. Follow these Right ✅ vs Wrong ❌ rules.

✅ What TO Do (Right Practices)

1. Clean Early Morning or Evening

Wash panels before 8 AM or after sunset when the glass is cool using normal clean water.

2. Use Soft Microfiber Mop / Sponge

Gently wipe dust every 10–15 days using a soft microfiber cloth or soft-bristle solar brush.

3. Trim Nearby Trees & Check Inverter

Keep panels shadow-free and check daily kWh readings on your inverter screen.

❌ What NOT To Do (Wrong Practices)

1. Never Pour Cold Water at Hot Noon

Spraying cold water on scorching hot glass at midday causes thermal shock and micro-cracks.

2. Never Stand or Walk on Panels

Stepping or leaning on panels creates invisible cell fractures that permanently reduce power.

3. Avoid Harsh Soaps & Metal Scrapers

Abrasive brushes, hard borewell salt water, or detergents scratch the anti-reflective glass coating.

SOLARSMARTGUIDER – PROJECT REPORT

Exhibition: RSBVP 2026–27

Project Category: Software / Digital Educational Model

Project Title: SolarSmartGuider – Smart Solar Energy Calculator & Sustainability Dashboard

Created by: Ajitesh Thawait (Roll No: 12127, Class 12 - A PCM) & Nikhil Dhruwe (Roll No: 12126, Class 12 - A PCM)

1. Introduction

SolarSmartGuider is an educational software platform designed to explain solar energy, estimate household solar requirements and promote renewable energy awareness.

2. Problem Statement

People may lack simple information about solar energy, system capacity, electricity generation and potential savings. This can make it difficult to understand whether rooftop solar may be suitable for their needs.

3. Proposed Solution

The platform accepts monthly electricity bill and optional roof area, providing educational estimates, currency conversion, graphs and explanations.

4. Scientific Principle

The project explains the photovoltaic effect, semiconductor materials, p-n junctions, electron-hole pair generation and the conversion of DC electricity into AC through an inverter.

5. Practical Applications

Its educational applications include homes, schools, agriculture, street lighting, healthcare facilities and industries.

6. Sustainability and Innovation

The project promotes renewable energy awareness, informed energy choices, environmental conservation and the vision of a sustainable and Viksit Bharat.

7. Technology Used

HTML for structure, CSS for design and JavaScript for calculations, charts and interactions.

8. Limitations

The current project uses illustrative assumptions and user inputs. It does not collect live solar measurements or provide a professional installation quotation.

9. Future Scope

Possible improvements include verified local weather data, location-based estimates, live solar monitoring and more detailed financial analysis.

10. Conclusion

SolarSmartGuider demonstrates how a simple digital tool can make renewable energy concepts easier to understand and encourage informed interest in clean energy technology.
